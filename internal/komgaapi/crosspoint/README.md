Mangarr

Read your Mangarr library on this reader. Each chapter is downloaded with
its pages already sized and shaded for the screen, and starts with a cover
page that becomes its tile on Home.

Setup (once, from a phone or computer):
1. In Mangarr, make a reading-app device key (My account, Reading apps).
2. Turn on File Transfer on the reader and open its web page.
3. Open Plugins, then Mangarr. Enter the Mangarr address with the reading
   app port (http://host:25600), the key, and this reader's screen size.
   Save, then Test.

Reading:
- Home, Plugins, Mangarr. Pick Continue reading, Recently added or
  Library A to Z. Each row is the chapter to read next in its title.
- Fetch downloads it to /Manga.
- When you leave the chapter or the reader sleeps, your progress is sent
  to Mangarr. Reaching the last page marks the chapter read.

Webtoons are left out, since long strips read badly on e-ink. Turn them on
in the plugin's settings if you want them.
