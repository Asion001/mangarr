# Code of conduct

mangarr is a small project run in people's spare time. Everyone who takes
part in its issues, pull requests and discussions is expected to help keep it
a friendly place to ask questions and to work together.

## What we expect

- Be kind and patient, especially with people who are new to self-hosting
  or to the code.
- Disagree with ideas, not with people. Explain your reasons, and accept that
  a maintainer may decide differently.
- Assume good intent, and ask before you assume otherwise.
- Keep discussions on topic, and keep other people's personal details,
  libraries and servers out of public threads.

## What isn't accepted

- Harassment, insults, slurs, or personal attacks of any kind.
- Sexual language or imagery aimed at people.
- Publishing someone's private information without their permission.
- Repeatedly pushing a point after a maintainer has asked you to stop.
- Asking for, or sharing, ways to get paid or copyrighted content without
  paying for it.

## Reporting

If someone's behaviour bothers you, report it privately: open the ⋯ menu on
the comment, issue or pull request, choose **Report content**, then **Report
to repository admins**. Only the maintainers see the report, and they will
treat it confidentially. For security problems, see [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md)
instead.

## What happens

Maintainers may edit or remove comments, close or lock threads, and block
people from the repository, depending on what happened and whether it
happened before. They'll explain the decision to the person involved when
it's safe to do so.

This code is adapted in spirit from the
[Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org).
